# Results

Before-and-after results from people who used these skills on their own CI.
There are no rows yet.

A row needs a public pull request whose description or comments show the runs
before and after, measured the ci-measure way: at least 5 comparable runs a
side, the median and the tail, the tests that ran compared by name, and the
verdict as `compare` printed it, with its run count. Slower and "no measurable change" results
are welcome too.
Open a pull request adding your row; a maintainer checks the link before
merging it.
